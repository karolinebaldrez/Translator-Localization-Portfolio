# 03 — Transcreation Case Study

## 📌 Project Overview

This case study demonstrates a **transcreation** process for a fictional lifestyle brand entering the Brazilian market.

Unlike traditional translation, transcreation focuses on preserving the **emotion, persuasive intent, brand personality, and cultural impact** of the original message.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objective is to adapt an English marketing campaign for a Brazilian audience while maintaining:

- Brand identity
- Emotional impact
- Marketing intent
- Tone of voice
- Cultural relevance
- Naturalness

The goal is not to reproduce the original wording, but to recreate its intended effect.

---

## 🌎 Language Direction

**Source Language:** English (US)

**Target Language:** Brazilian Portuguese

**Target Market:** Brazil 🇧🇷

**Industry:** Lifestyle / Fashion

---

# 1. Original Campaign

### Brand

**Fictional Brand:** Nocturne

### Campaign

> **Own the night.**  
> Dress boldly. Live freely.  
> Discover the collection made for those who refuse to blend in.

### Campaign Goal

The campaign positions the brand as:

- Bold
- Independent
- Confident
- Modern
- Non-conformist

The language should feel aspirational rather than purely descriptive.

---

# 2. Literal Translation

A literal translation could be:

> **Possua a noite.**  
> Vista-se corajosamente. Viva livremente.  
> Descubra a coleção feita para aqueles que se recusam a se misturar.

Although the meaning can be understood, the result sounds unnatural and does not preserve the original advertising impact.

---

# 3. Transcreation Analysis

## "Own the night."

The expression "own the night" is idiomatic and highly promotional.

A literal translation such as:

> "Possua a noite."

does not work naturally in Brazilian Portuguese.

The phrase communicates a broader concept of **confidence, presence, and taking control of the moment**.

### Transcreated version:

> **A noite é sua.**

This version is short, natural, memorable, and maintains the original emotional impact.

---

## "Dress boldly."

The word "boldly" communicates confidence and a willingness to stand out.

Possible translations include:

- Vista-se com ousadia.
- Vista-se sem medo.
- Ouse no seu estilo.

For a fashion campaign, **"Ouse no seu estilo"** creates a more natural and engaging advertising message.

---

## "Live freely."

A literal translation would be:

> Viva livremente.

While grammatically correct, this sounds more formal than the original.

A more natural advertising adaptation is:

> **Viva do seu jeito.**

This preserves the idea of freedom while making the message more emotionally relevant to Brazilian audiences.

---

## "Refuse to blend in."

The phrase describes someone who does not want to conform or disappear into the crowd.

A literal translation would sound unnatural:

> Recuse-se a se misturar.

A more effective transcreation is:

> **Para quem nasceu para se destacar.**

This preserves the positioning of individuality while creating stronger marketing language.

---

# 4. Final Transcreated Campaign

> ## **A noite é sua.**
>
> **Ouse no seu estilo. Viva do seu jeito.**
>
> Descubra a coleção para quem nasceu para se destacar.

---

# 6. Creative Decisions

### Emotional Impact

The final version prioritizes the emotional response of the target audience rather than maintaining the exact sentence structure.

### Brand Voice

The language remains:

- Confident
- Modern
- Bold
- Aspirational

### Cultural Naturalness

Expressions were selected based on how Brazilian Portuguese is naturally used in advertising and fashion communication.

### Brevity

Marketing copy benefits from short, memorable phrases.

The localized campaign therefore avoids unnecessary literal structures.

# 🏆 Final Assessment

This case demonstrates the difference between **translation and transcreation**.

A translator may ask:

> "How can I translate this sentence?"

A transcreator asks:

> "How can I make the Brazilian audience feel what the original audience was supposed to feel?"

That distinction is particularly important in:

- Advertising
- Marketing
- Fashion
- Entertainment
- Social media
- Branding
- Creative campaigns

---

## Key Takeaway

> **Translation preserves meaning. Transcreation preserves impact.**
