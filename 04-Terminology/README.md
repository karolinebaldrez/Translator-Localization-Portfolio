# 04 — Terminology Management

## 📌 Project Overview

This case study demonstrates the creation of a bilingual terminology glossary for a fictional digital product.

Terminology management is essential for maintaining **consistency, accuracy, and brand alignment** across localized content.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objective is to establish approved terminology for a digital product localized from English into Brazilian Portuguese.

The glossary provides translators and linguistic reviewers with:

- Approved translations
- Alternative terms
- Terms to avoid
- Context
- Usage notes
- Consistency guidelines

---

## 🌎 Language Pair

**Source Language:** English (US)

**Target Language:** Brazilian Portuguese (PT-BR)

**Domain:** Software / Mobile Application

## 🚫 Terms to Avoid

Certain literal translations may be understandable but should not be used when a more natural and established term exists.

### Example 1

**Source:** Sign in

❌ Logar

✅ Entrar

Reason:

"Logar" is commonly understood but is generally less appropriate for polished consumer-facing UI.

---

### Example 2

**Source:** Track order

❌ Rastrear pedido

✅ Acompanhar pedido

Reason:

"Acompanhar pedido" provides a more natural UX expression for e-commerce interfaces.

---

### Example 3

**Source:** Settings

❌ Definições

✅ Configurações

Reason:

"Configurações" is the preferred term for Brazilian Portuguese software interfaces.

# 🧠 Terminology Management Principles

## Consistency

The same source concept should use the same approved translation throughout the product unless context requires otherwise.

## Context

Terminology should always be evaluated within its specific product and linguistic context.

## User Experience

Terms used in interfaces should be concise, recognizable, and natural to the target audience.

## Brand Alignment

Approved terminology should reflect the product's established voice and communication style.

## Continuous Maintenance

Glossaries should evolve as products, features, and terminology change.

---

# 📊 Terminology QA Checklist

Before approving a localized string, reviewers should verify:

- [ ] Approved terminology is used
- [ ] Terms to avoid are not present
- [ ] Terminology is consistent with previous content
- [ ] Context is appropriate
- [ ] The term is natural for Brazilian Portuguese users
- [ ] Product-specific terminology follows the glossary
- [ ] UI terminology is concise and consistent

---

# 🏆 Final Assessment

A terminology glossary provides a centralized linguistic reference that helps translators, reviewers, and localization teams maintain consistency across large volumes of content.

Effective terminology management improves:

**Consistency + Quality + Efficiency + User Experience**
