# 02 — Localization Case Study

## 📌 Project Overview

This case study demonstrates the localization of a fictional US-based digital product for the **Brazilian market**.

The objective is to adapt the content beyond literal translation, taking into consideration:

- Brazilian Portuguese conventions
- Cultural context
- User expectations
- Currency
- Date and time formats
- Tone of voice
- UX writing
- Local terminology

---

## 🎯 Objective

The goal is to demonstrate the difference between **translation and localization**.

A successful localization should preserve the original intent while making the product feel as though it was originally created for the target market.

---

## 🌎 Localization Direction

**Source Market:** United States 🇺🇸

**Target Market:** Brazil 🇧🇷

**Source Language:** English (US)

**Target Language:** Brazilian Portuguese (PT-BR)

**Product:** Mobile shopping application

---

# 1. Original Content

### Promotional Banner

> **Summer Sale — Up to 50% Off**  
> Shop now and get free shipping on orders over $75.  
> Offer valid through July 31.

### Checkout Message

> Your order will arrive between August 5 and August 8.

### Account Message

> Sign up today and receive $10 off your first purchase.

---

# 2. Literal Translation

A literal translation could look like this:

> **Promoção de Verão — Até 50% de desconto**  
> Compre agora e ganhe frete grátis em pedidos acima de US$ 75.  
> Oferta válida até 31 de julho.

> Seu pedido chegará entre 5 e 8 de agosto.

> Cadastre-se hoje e receba US$ 10 de desconto na sua primeira compra.

While this translation is understandable, it does not fully localize the experience for Brazilian users.

---

# 3. Localization Analysis

## 🌤️ Seasonal Context

The campaign uses **"Summer Sale"**.

The US summer occurs approximately from June through August, while Brazil is in the Southern Hemisphere and experiences winter during this period.

A direct translation could therefore create a cultural mismatch.

### Recommended adaptation

Instead of:

> Promoção de Verão

Use a neutral campaign concept such as:

> **Oferta Especial — Até 50% de desconto**

This preserves the promotional intent without introducing a seasonal reference that conflicts with the Brazilian market.

---

## 💰 Currency

The original promotion uses:

> $75

For a Brazilian audience, displaying the price in US dollars creates unnecessary friction.

The localized version should use **Brazilian reais (BRL)** when the product is actually sold in Brazil.

Example:

> Frete grátis em pedidos acima de **R$ 350**

The exact value should be determined by the business based on pricing and currency conversion rules.

---

## 📅 Date Format

The US format may use:

> July 31

Brazilian interfaces generally use:

> 31 de julho

Therefore:

**US:** July 31  
**Brazil:** 31 de julho

---

## 📦 Delivery Information

Original:

> Your order will arrive between August 5 and August 8.

Localized:

> Seu pedido chegará entre **5 e 8 de agosto**.

The date structure is adapted to Brazilian Portuguese while preserving the original meaning.

---

## 🎁 Promotional Incentive

Original:

> Receive $10 off your first purchase.

A literal translation would preserve the dollar amount.

For a Brazilian localized experience, the incentive should be represented in **BRL** according to the local commercial strategy.

Example:

> **Cadastre-se hoje e ganhe R$ 50 de desconto na sua primeira compra.**

The value is an example and would need to be validated against the actual promotion.

---

# 4. Final Localized Version

### Promotional Banner

> **Oferta Especial — Até 50% de desconto**  
> Compre agora e ganhe frete grátis em pedidos acima de **R$ 350**.  
> Oferta válida até **31 de julho**.

### Checkout Message

> Seu pedido chegará entre **5 e 8 de agosto**.

### Account Message

> **Cadastre-se hoje e ganhe R$ 50 de desconto na sua primeira compra.**

---

# 5. Localization Decisions

| Element | Source | Localized Version | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seasonal campaign | Summer Sale | Oferta Especial | Avoids Southern Hemisphere mismatch |
| Currency | USD | BRL | Local market convention |
| Date | July 31 | 31 de julho | Brazilian date convention |
| Delivery dates | August 5–8 | 5 a 8 de agosto | Natural PT-BR formatting |
| Discount | $10 | R$ 50 | Example of local commercial adaptation |
| Tone | Direct promotional English | Natural Brazilian Portuguese | UX and cultural adaptation |

---

# 6. Localization Principles Applied

### Cultural Adaptation

Content was adapted to avoid assumptions based on the source market.

### Currency Localization

Prices were adapted to the target market's currency.

### Date Localization

Dates were reformatted according to Brazilian conventions.

### UX Writing

The localized copy prioritizes clarity, brevity, and natural language.

### Commercial Adaptation

Promotional elements were adapted to fit the expectations of Brazilian consumers.

---

# 🏆 Final Assessment

Localization is not simply replacing words from one language with another.

It requires understanding:

**Language + Culture + Market + User Experience + Business Context**

The final version maintains the original commercial intent while creating a more natural experience for Brazilian users.

---

## Key Takeaway

> **Translation transfers meaning. Localization adapts the experience.**
