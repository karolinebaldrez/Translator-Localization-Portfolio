# 01 — Translation Quality Assurance

## 📌 Project Overview

This case study demonstrates a structured **Translation Quality Assurance (TQA)** process for an English → Brazilian Portuguese translation.

The objective is to identify, classify, and correct linguistic issues while evaluating the overall quality of the translated content.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The goal of this project is to demonstrate how a professional linguistic reviewer can:

- Identify translation errors
- Classify different error types
- Assess error severity
- Provide appropriate corrections
- Explain linguistic decisions
- Evaluate overall translation quality

---

## 🌎 Language Pair

**Source Language:** English (US)

**Target Language:** Brazilian Portuguese (PT-BR)

**Domain:** E-commerce / Digital Product

---

## 📝 Source Text

> Make your shopping experience easier with our new mobile app.  
> Save your favorite products, track your orders in real time, and get personalized recommendations based on your interests.

## ✏️ Recommended Translation

> Facilite sua experiência de compra com nosso novo aplicativo para celular.  
> Salve seus produtos favoritos, acompanhe seus pedidos em tempo real e receba recomendações personalizadas com base nos seus interesses.

---

## 🧠 Linguistic Rationale

### "shopping experience" → "experiência de compra"

While "experiência de compras" is understandable, **"experiência de compra"** is more natural in Brazilian Portuguese when referring to the overall customer journey.

### "mobile app" → "aplicativo para celular"

"Aplicativo móvel" is a valid translation, but "aplicativo para celular" is often more natural and user-oriented in Brazilian Portuguese.

### "track your orders" → "acompanhe seus pedidos"

In an e-commerce context, **"acompanhar um pedido"** is the conventional expression in Brazilian Portuguese.

The literal translation "rastrear seus pedidos" is understandable, but can sound more associated with package tracking systems than with the broader customer experience.

---

## 📊 Overall QA Assessment

**Overall Quality:** Good

**Main Issues:** Fluency, localization, and naturalness

**Critical Errors:** 0

**Major Errors:** 0

**Minor Issues:** 3

The initial translation successfully communicates the original meaning. However, several expressions can be improved to sound more natural and localized for Brazilian Portuguese users.

---

## 🏆 Final Assessment

This review demonstrates that translation quality goes beyond grammatical accuracy.

A high-quality localized translation should consider:

**Accuracy + Fluency + Context + Terminology + Cultural Appropriateness + User Experience**

The final version preserves the original message while providing a more natural experience for Brazilian Portuguese users.
