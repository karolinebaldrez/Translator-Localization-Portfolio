---

## Error Severity Framework

### Critical

Errors that:

- Change the intended meaning
- Create serious misinformation
- Cause significant legal, financial, or safety risks
- Make the content unusable

### Major

Errors that:

- Significantly affect meaning
- Create substantial confusion
- Damage the user experience
- Introduce important terminology or contextual problems

### Minor

Errors that:

- Affect naturalness
- Involve stylistic inconsistencies
- Could be improved without changing the core meaning

---

## Reviewer Conclusion

The translation is suitable for publication after minor linguistic adjustments.

The most important improvements involve replacing literal translations with expressions that are more commonly used by Brazilian Portuguese speakers in an e-commerce context.

This demonstrates the importance of evaluating not only **semantic accuracy**, but also **naturalness, localization, and user experience**.
